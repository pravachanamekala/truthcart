# Returns and Refunds — FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY

## Standard return window
Eligible unused products may be requested for return within 7 calendar days of delivery. The request must include the order reference and product condition details.

## Opened packages
Opening outer packaging alone does not automatically establish eligibility. Items must be complete and undamaged. Hygiene-sealed products and products marked final-sale are excluded; the catalogue does not currently identify which products are in those categories, so eligibility must be checked with support.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds are initiated within 5–7 business days after the returned item passes inspection. Bank processing time may add extra time.

## Exceptions
This demo policy does not specify rules for damaged-on-arrival items, digital goods, or products without an order reference. Do not infer eligibility for these cases.
