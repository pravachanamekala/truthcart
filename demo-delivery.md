# Delivery — FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY

## Estimated delivery
Use the delivery estimate on an individual product record when present. Estimates are illustrative and are not live carrier tracking.

## Missing estimates
If a product has no delivery estimate, say that the catalogue does not specify one. Do not substitute another product’s estimate.

## Delivery exceptions
This demo policy does not specify remote-area surcharges, same-day delivery, or guaranteed delivery dates.
