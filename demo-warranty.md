# Warranty — FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY

## Product warranty
Warranty coverage and duration are product-specific. Use the product record when a warranty field is explicitly populated. A blank warranty field means the catalogue does not specify the term; it does not prove that the product has no warranty.

## Claim process
For a warranty claim, retain proof of purchase and contact demo support with the product ID and a description of the issue.

## Exclusions
This document does not specify accidental-damage coverage, battery degradation coverage, or repair turnaround times. Do not promise coverage for these situations.
