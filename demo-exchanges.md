# Exchanges and Eligibility — FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY

## Exchange requests
Exchange requests may be submitted within 7 calendar days of delivery for eligible unused products, subject to inspection and stock availability.

## Conditions
The product must be complete and undamaged. The catalogue does not provide live replacement stock or a definitive eligibility flag for each item.

## Unsupported scenarios
This policy does not specify exchanges for sale items, personalised items, or items missing original accessories. Ask support rather than guessing.
